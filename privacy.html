Privacy Policy

Dashboard TikTok · Last updated: 2026-03-21

1. Who we are

This app is a personal / internal tool that connects your TikTok accounts via TikTok Login Kit to display aggregated analytics (followers, likes, video stats) in one dashboard.

2. Data we access

With your consent through TikTok OAuth, we may receive:

  • Basic profile information (e.g. open_id, display name, avatar if granted)
  • Account statistics (e.g. follower count, likes count, video count)
  • Public video metadata and engagement metrics (views, likes, comments, shares)

We do not request permission to post, message, or modify content on your behalf.

3. How we use data

Data is used only to show analytics in your dashboard, refresh OAuth tokens, and keep historical snapshots you request. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

4. Storage

Access and refresh tokens and metric snapshots are stored on systems you control (local or your private server). Tokens are not published publicly.

5. Sharing

We do not share TikTok account data with third parties, except as required to operate the TikTok API connection itself (TikTok / ByteDance as the platform provider).

6. Retention

Data is kept while you use the app. You can disconnect an account (revoke OAuth) and delete local token/metric files at any time.

7. Your choices

You may revoke access in TikTok settings (authorized apps / manage permissions) and stop using this dashboard. After revoke, API access ends; delete local files to remove stored copies.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about this app, contact the app operator who registered the TikTok developer application.
